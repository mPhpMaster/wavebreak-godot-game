## Changes

- Every puzzle room is much harder. Ember Hall: chained braziers, star and cross plaques, a burning fuse. Gust Room: numbered plates in order across rings of wind. Mirror Room: two lamps, splitters, linked mirrors. Aqueduct Room: 7x7 grid, several spouts and basins, leaks, bolted and chained pipes.
- Ice Hall (numbered sockets, cracking ice, sliding blocks), Weighing Room (tight crate pushing, iron crates, ordered plates) and Lamp Room (padlocked levers, counted pulls) are tighter.
- Bell Room has clue tablets, Counting Room has limited moves and a changing counterweight, Lantern Path has fences, a moon tile and a stall timer.
- Difficulty climbs faster; puzzle maps have three to five rooms.
- Puzzle boons: each solved room gives the whole team a boon (speed, air jump, jump height, dash, health and heal, armour). Boons stack until the puzzle map or the gauntlet ends.
- Puzzle signs hang straight on the wall, so Arabic text is no longer cut.
- Co-op protocol 18: update every player together.
