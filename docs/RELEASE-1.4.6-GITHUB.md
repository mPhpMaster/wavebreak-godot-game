## Changes

- Corrected Nori's shotgun orientation in world space: swimming no longer flips the receiver and grips upside down. The barrel sits clear of the face, with a rear trigger grip and forward support grip. Both arms reach these grips; standing and swimming were rendered separately.
- The actual first Reef phase now summons the licensed downloaded Leviathan. An inherited generic boss unlock gate previously allowed a different boss to replace it; the old direct-spawn fixture missed that selection path.
- Enlarged the Leviathan model fourfold (about 31 × 24 × 32 metres in its horizontal swimming pose). Its collider uses a 15-metre radius and 32-metre height envelope. Attack reach, telegraphs, player-weapon acquisition and body-surface hit tests account for this size.
- Added underwater volume-aware spawn placement, safe team clearance and centre-based depth pursuit for the huge boss. Cave Shark remains an independent cave guardian.
- Preserved the mild nonstacking shotgun recoil and dive controls from 1.4.5.
- Co-op protocol 12: update every player together.
