# New-player acceptance session

Run with three participants unfamiliar with these controls. Use both mouse and
keyboard and a controller, then repeat as a two-player co-op session. Collect
consent before recording; this document does not authorize contacting anyone.

## Route and observations

1. Launch Classic Play after previously selecting a City expedition. Record
   first map, time until movement and any unexplained loading stall.
2. Choose Reef, choose an alternate unlocked character, then return and choose
   Nori. Record whether map/character/back navigation is understood.
3. Swim up/down, steer through a dash, breach and return underwater. Ask the
   player to describe the dash direction, duration and recoil before explaining.
4. Find the cave, identify entrance/exit landmarks, collect a resource and
   explain the guardian/boss goal. Record wrong turns and uncollectible-looking
   objects that the player expects to collect.
5. Choose City and Vesper. Without coaching, attach each hook, release one,
   boost with Jump and Dash, then strike a core. Repeat hold/toggle modes.
6. In co-op, open both level-up layouts during combat and a rest stop. Check
   movement and card selection. Review team DPS and the scoreboard together.
7. Stand on a platform, separate the two players and observe enemy pursuit and
   spawn placement. Record stuck enemies and unexplained damage/deaths.

## Metrics

Record first-action time, time to first successful hook/boost/core hit, cave
completion time, wrong turns, missed input attempts, stuck enemies, unexpected
hits/deaths, visible hitches and whether each participant correctly predicts
their next action. Ask for movement, combat, readability and sound ratings 1–5.

## Exit questions

- What did you think the next goal was?
- Which input or attack felt unreliable, and what did you expect instead?
- Where did you lose your sense of direction?
- Which sound or visual cue confirmed a successful hit or reward?
- Would you replay, and what single change would matter most?

## Completion rule

Store participant observations separately from automated test logs. A prepared
script or passing automation does not constitute completed participant testing
or human listening acceptance. Those results are currently pending.
